What is holistic architecture?

add

Holistic architecture is an approach that considers the building as a whole, living system. It integrates the structure, its environment, the interiors, and the psychological impact on occupants into a single, unified design narrative. We prioritize harmony between form, function, and nature.

Do you manage government approvals and permits?

add

Yes, INFINITE provides full end-to-end management, including all necessary approvals from local authorities (DDA, Dubai Municipality, Civil Defence, etc.). Our team handles the technical documentation and liaison to ensure a seamless project commencement.

What is the typical timeline for a fit-out project?

add

Timelines vary based on scope and scale. A typical commercial or luxury residential fit-out can range from 12 to 24 weeks. During the initial consultation, we provide a detailed project roadmap with key milestones from design freeze to handover.

How do you handle budget management?

add

We operate with total transparency. Budgets are defined at the conceptual stage and tracked meticulously through our 'Technical Integrity' protocol. We provide detailed BOQs (Bill of Quantities) and regular financial updates to avoid any surprises.

Can I visit projects currently under construction?

add

We encourage it. Seeing our craftsmanship in person is the best way to understand our quality standards. Site visits can be arranged upon request, subject to safety protocols and client privacy agreements.
