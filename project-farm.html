PROJECT 04 // ITALY

Farm Villa

THE NATURAL CONTEXT

"The architecture does not sit upon the landscape, it emerges from it—a silent witness to the passage of time."

Situated amidst the rolling hills of the countryside, the Farm Villa represents a masterclass in contextual integration. The project began with a deep study of the site's topographical nuances, ensuring that the footprint of the new structure honored the existing olive groves and natural slopes.

The orientation maximizes passive solar gain while frame-specific apertures act as living canvases, capturing the seasonal transitions of the valley below. Here, the boundaries between interior comfort and exterior wildness are intentionally blurred.

RECLAIMED TIMBER

Sourced from local heritage structures, the structural beams retain their original patina, grounding the modern interior with historical weight.

QUARRIED STONE

Hand-cut local limestone creates a monolithic thermal mass that regulates temperature while echoing the ancient geology of the region.

STRUCTURAL GLASS

High-performance triple-glazing systems allow for massive spans with minimal framing, bringing the sky into the living space.
