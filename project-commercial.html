Commercial Villa

An exercise in architectural transparency and organic integration.

Completed

Redefining the boundary between private luxury and professional functionality.

The Commercial Villa project in Dubai stands as a testament to INFINITE's philosophy of "Invisible Boundaries." Tasked with creating a space that functions equally as a high-stakes meeting environment and a serene executive retreat, we utilized a tiered architectural language.

The structure maximizes the use of double-height glass facades to invite the Dubai skyline inside, while deep overhangs provide natural shading and intimate terrace spaces layered with lush, integrated greenery.

Architectural Concept

01 / FORM & FUNCTION

Luminous Transparency

Using floor-to-ceiling high-performance glazing, the villa maintains a constant dialogue with its environment. The glass is treated with a subtle reflective coating that preserves daytime privacy while offering panoramic views of the water.

Vertical Oasis

Vegetation is not an ornament but a structural component. Trailing plants soften the hard edges of the concrete cantilevers, providing natural cooling and a biophilic connection for the inhabitants.
