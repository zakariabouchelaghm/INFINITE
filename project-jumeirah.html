Private Residence | Jumeirah

Jumeirah Villa: A Coastal Sanctuary

Where Mediterranean tradition meets modern precision. A holistic study in light, texture, and the rhythm of the Arabian coast.

The Narrative

Nestled in the heart of Jumeirah, this project redefines coastal luxury through a lens of minimalist architectural purity. The villa serves as a bridge between the intense desert sun and the cooling influence of the sea.

LOCATION

Jumeirah, Dubai

AREA

1,250 SQ.M

TYPE

Holistic Fit-Out & Architectural Design

TEXTURE & FORM: REFINED WHITE PLASTER

AQUATIC RHYTHM: THE INFINITY PERIPHERY

PHILOSOPHY

Human-Centric Planning

At INFINITE, we design for the human soul. Every corridor, transition, and void in the Jumeirah Villa has been calibrated to foster a sense of instinctive movement and effortless belonging. We prioritize the flow of natural light as a primary building material, ensuring that spaces breathe with the inhabitants.
